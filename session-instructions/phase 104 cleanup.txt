## Intended visual behavior

The arcs should be drawn as grip marks inside the selected widget near the bottom-right corner.

They should:

- live inside the widget bounds
- sit near the bottom-right resize handle
- connect visually between the right selection edge and the bottom selection edge
- suggest the usable resize corner region inside that corner
- read as grip lines, not as a rounded outer border

The orientation should match the intended “upper-left portion of the bottom-right corner grip area.”

In practice, this means:

- the arcs should visually open toward the interior/top-left of the widget
- the curve should not bulge the wrong way
- the arcs should not look like an outside rounded frame corner
